**The cleanest unadulterated path is the official PyPI wheel.** `pip install openswmm==6.0.0a3` pulls the upstream-built binary from the alpha.3 release — nothing patched, nothing forked. I installed it and ran your VJ smoke test through it:

All four SWMM6 options honored, VJ table present, `HAS_2D` and `HAS_GEOPACKAGE` both True. API is `openswmm.engine.run(inp, rpt, out)`. That's a working alternate engine today, no build step.

**The licensing point that makes this matter: compiling unmodified source is not "modifying files."** §4(b) attaches to changed *files*, not to the act of building. So a clean checkout of `v6.0.0-alpha.3` built native, or the wheel, needs only §4(a), (c), (d) — license copy, retained headers, NOTICE. No per-file change notices, no fork to maintain.

**Only your browser build is adulterated.** The three Emscripten patches (PluginFactory, IOThread, OpenMP dedup) are what pull you into §4(b), and CMake exposes no option that removes them — the option list is tests/benchmarks/install/Python/GeoPackage/2D/GPU, nothing for plugins or threads. Two honest ways out: upstream the patches to `swmm6_rel` (exactly the contribution the alpha program is asking for, and then everyone gets browser SWMM6 from clean source), or try replacing them with build flags — pthread build + COOP/COEP for IOThread, `--allow-multiple-definition` for the symbol collision. That second one is a hypothesis, not a result; it needs a round-trip `.out` diff against the wheel before you'd claim parity.

Either way, keep the wheel installed. It's the reference oracle that lets you *show* your WASM build is faithful instead of asserting it — which is the thing your own testing discipline demands anyway.

Corinne's public statement matches `swmm6_rel`. The workable rule is to honour whatever ships *inside* the artifact you redistribute and record its provenance — but this is worth putting through her feedback form rather than guessing at. It's a genuinely useful alpha-tester finding, and it's the kind of thing she was asking for.
