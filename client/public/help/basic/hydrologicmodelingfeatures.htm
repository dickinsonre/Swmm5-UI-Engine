Example Study Area

    

In this tutorial we will model the drainage system serving a 12 acre residential area. The system layout is shown below and consists of subcatchment areas S1 through S3 Note, storm sewer conduits C1 through C4, and conduit junctions J1 through J4. The system discharges to a creek at the point labeled Out1. We will first go through the steps of creating the objects shown in this diagram on SWMM's Study Area Map and setting the various properties of these objects. Then we will simulate the water quantity and quality response to a 3-inch, 6-hour rainfall event, as well as a continuous, multi-year record.
