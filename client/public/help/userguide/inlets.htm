Inlets

    

Street inlets are curb and gutter openings that convey runoff from streets into below-ground sewers. Drop inlets serve a similar purpose for trapezoidal channels. SWMM can compute the amount of flow captured by inlets and sent to designated sewer nodes using the FHWA HEC-22 methodology. The type, sizing, and spacing of street inlets will determine if the spread and depth of water on roadways can be maintained at acceptable levels.

 

To analyze street drainage with SWMM a site is represented as a dual drainage system consisting of both street conduits along the ground surface and sewer conduits below it.  An inlet structure will divert some portion of the street flow it sees into a designated node of the sewer system with the rest being bypassed to downstream streets. When an inlet’s sewer node reaches its full depth any excess flow that would cause it to flood is sent back through the inlet and onto the street.
